## Additional lifetime/receiver contract for runtime consumers

These are not extra Router ALPN handlers, but are startup-owned resources later consumers need to preserve:

- `endpoint`, `router`, `gossip`, `blob_store` are returned or bound at `main.rs:1860-88`; `router` is the started router.
- `whisper`: `WhisperBuilder::new(endpoint.clone(), secret_key.clone())`, `protocol_handler()`, then `spawn()` at `1215-18`; router registration `1350`. Expose `whisper_handle` and `whisper_events_rx: Arc<tokio::sync::Mutex<Whisper event receiver>>` (wrapped at 1795; returned at 1874-75).

- `file_offer_registry` (969), `transfer_store` (949), `outbound_item_labels` (950-52) are shared file-transfer state; later GUI adoption needs the same shared instances, not new registries.
- Friend manager, backfill client, whisper, inbox, and call actor handles/receivers are the specific actor/channel pairs detailed in table. Preserve receiver wrappers and types; do not drop them when moving construction.
- Screen-share event/media/audio channels are feature-gated and wired into `app.calls_state` (2235-47); `screen_share_events_tx` is also retained there.
- Blob provider event receiver is consumed by `spawn_outbound_provider_consumer` at 1135-42. The resulting task handle is not retained in this path; a runtime extraction should explicitly own its task lifetime.
